# ha-tv-kit · EXAMPLE debloat list for a TCL Google TV (one model, one firmware, one region).
# It is an example to compare against, not a list to apply blindly. Other TCL models, and other brands
# (Hisense, Sony, Philips, Xiaomi, ...), have different package names. Check every line against YOUR
# TV's package list (tv/backups/<name>/packages-before.txt) and leave out anything you're unsure of.
# Everything here is reversible with restore.ps1 / restore.sh.
#
# Reference result: on two TCL Google TVs (Android 14) every Tier 1 and Tier 2 line below was disabled,
# plus two unused factory streaming apps (18 in all). After a restart, the inputs, Chromecast, AirPlay
# and remote services were still running. Disabling ...tv.dreamx leaves the screensaver
# blank until you set Aerial Views (docs/02, Step 7).
